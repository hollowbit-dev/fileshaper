Why Fileshaper exists

Shrinking a video for Discord, opening an iPhone photo on Windows, signing a PDF: almost everyone has to do these things. The usual answer is a website that wants you to upload your files, wait in a queue and pay to remove a watermark.

That felt wrong for jobs a normal computer can do in seconds. Fileshaper is the answer: one app for video, images, PDF and audio that runs entirely on your own computer.

It ships with installers for Windows and Mac, this website, and 21 automated tests that drive the real app to check every tool works.

What Hollowbit sticks to

No ads
Not in the app, not on the website.
No accounts
You shouldn't need to sign up to convert a file.
No tracking
No analytics and no data collection.
Open source
Every line of code is public.

How it's built

Electron

One codebase for Windows and Mac

FFmpeg

Video, audio and image processing

pdf-lib and PDF.js

Editing, signing and drawing PDFs

Two-pass encoding

Lands just under a target file size

Playwright

21 end-to-end tests on the real app

No network calls

Works with the Wi-Fi off
