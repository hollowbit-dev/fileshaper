Changelog

Every release of Fileshaper, newest first.

1.2.011 November 2026

Filesmith is now Fileshaper

  • New name: Filesmith is now Fileshaper. Another product already used the old name, so this one is unmistakably yours. Your settings and history carry over.
  • Free online tools on the website: convert HEIC to JPG, compress images, merge, sign, split and compress PDFs, compress videos and make GIFs right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
1.1.011 November 2026

Six languages, right-click and updates

  • The app is now in English, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Chinese.
  • Right-click any file and choose Open with Fileshaper (Windows), or Open With, Fileshaper (Mac).
  • Automatic updates: Windows installs new versions on its own; Mac shows a notice with a download link. You can turn update checks off in Settings.
  • Smoother motion: sliding controls, finished files flash and check off.
  • Licence and third-party notices are included in the app.
1.0.010 November 2026

First release

  • Video: compress to a size or quality, trim, make GIFs, extract audio, convert to MP4, remove sound.
  • Images: convert HEIC and other formats, resize, compress, turn images into PDFs.
  • PDF: sign and fill, merge, split, organize pages, compress.
  • Audio: convert, trim, even out volume.
  • Command bar (Ctrl K or ⌘K) that takes plain-language outcomes.
  • Dark and light appearance. Native sidebar material on macOS and Windows 11.
  • Installers for Windows and for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
