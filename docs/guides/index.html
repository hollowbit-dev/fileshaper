Guides

Plain answers to everyday file problems, each done on your own computer for free.

  • How to compress a video for DiscordShrink any video to fit Discord's 10 MB upload limit without Nitro and without uploading it to a website. Free, offline, works on Windows and Mac.
  • How to open HEIC photos on Windows (and convert them to JPG)iPhone photos are saved as HEIC, which many Windows apps can't open. Convert HEIC to JPG in bulk, free and offline, without uploading your photos.
  • How to sign a PDF without uploading itAdd a drawn or typed signature and the date to any PDF without sending your contract to a website. Free and offline on Windows and Mac.
  • How to merge PDF files offlineCombine several PDFs into one in any order, without uploading them. Free and offline on Windows and Mac, with no page limit.
  • How to make a GIF from a videoTurn any part of a video into a GIF: pick the moment, choose the size and smoothness, done. Free, offline, no watermark.
  • How to compress a PDF for email or uploadMake a PDF small enough to email or upload to a school or job portal, without sending it to a website. Free and offline on Windows and Mac.
