Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version: Fileshaper collects nothing. Your files never leave your computer.

The app

Fileshaper processes every file on your own computer. It has no account, no analytics and sends no crash reports. The only network request is an optional update check: once a day it asks GitHub for the latest version number, and sends nothing about you or your files. You can turn it off in Settings. Your settings and the list of files you've made are stored on your computer, and you can clear the history at any time.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Fonts are hosted here, not loaded from Google. Your language choice is remembered in your browser only.

Tips

If you leave a tip on Ko-fi, Ko-fi handles the payment under its own privacy policy. Fileshaper never sees your payment details.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
