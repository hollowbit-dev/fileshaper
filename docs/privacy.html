Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version: Filesmith collects nothing. Your files never leave your computer.

The app

Filesmith processes every file on your own computer. It makes no network requests, has no account and no analytics, and sends no crash reports. Your settings and the list of files you've made are stored in a file on your computer, and you can clear the history in the app at any time.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Fonts are hosted here, not loaded from Google. Your language choice is remembered in your browser only.

Tips

If you leave a tip on Ko-fi, Ko-fi handles the payment under its own privacy policy. Filesmith never sees your payment details.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
